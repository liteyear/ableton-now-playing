Free Mac now-playing widget for Ableton Live Session View and OBS, by Liteyear Design.

Download **Ableton-Now-Playing-Mac-v1.0.0.zip**, extract it, drag the app into Applications, and open it. The four-step wizard installs AbletonOSC and walks you through adding one fully styled OBS Browser source. No Terminal, Node installation, CSS editing, or separate dependency downloads required.

Includes Intel and Apple Silicon runtimes, deck selection, optional artist/title splitting, hide-on-stop, reconnect recovery, and a 20-second OBS test card. Use 1100 × 180 at 1 FPS in OBS.

Requires macOS 11+, Ableton Live 11+ with Session View, and OBS Browser source support. The app is unsigned; macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. See the included guide for setup and updates.
